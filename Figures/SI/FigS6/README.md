# Fig. S6 — merged single-mode dimer scans and spectra

The Command-28 figure is a balanced 2x2 composition:

- panels (a) and (b) show the full dimensionless spectral-distance scan
  `w=W1/nu0` for `J=+25 meV` and `J=-25 meV`, respectively, at
  `S=0.02, 0.05, 0.10`;
- panel (c) shows the actual `S=0.10`, `J=+25 meV`, `r=0.800` ED and SCBA
  spectra;
- panel (d) shows the actual `S=0.10`, `J=+25 meV`, `r=1.880` ED and SCBA
  spectra.

The upper panels share both axes.  Solid colored traces join calculated scan
points in increasing `r`; matching colored dashed lines are the calculated
`J=0` baselines.  Markers `c` and `d` on panel (a) identify the two spectra
below.  There is no inset, integer guide, or A--D cut catalog.

Generate the figure from the project root with:

```bash
python3 Figures/SI/FigS6/plot_FigS6.py
```

The stable output path remains `Figures/SI/FigS6/FigS6.pdf` even though the
manuscript labels it Fig. S6.

## Numerical source

The full scans contain 2,248 calculated points: the validated 2,214-point
base mesh plus 34 independently calculated revision-27 refined-`r`
points.  No scan values are interpolated, smoothed, filtered, fitted, or
downsampled.

Panel (c) is read from the checked spectrum archive
`Figures/common/dimer/single_mode/data/eta0p005/spectra/eta005_S0p1_Jp_r0p8.npz`.
The base scan contains the `r=1.880` point but the earlier spectral-cut
catalog did not archive its arrays.  Panel (d) is therefore regenerated by
the checked revision-27 solver with the same numerical settings:
`Delta x=0.002`, `N_vib=10`, `eta/nu0=0.005`, internal support `[-32,24]`,
and normalization/metric support `[-18,18]`.  Its recomputed `w` agrees with
the stored scan value to below `1e-12`.

For both spectral panels, `x=(E-E_B)/nu0` and `dE=nu0 dx`.  The plotted
density is

```text
p_x(x) = nu0 A(E_B + nu0 x) / integral_[x=-18..18] A(E) dE,
```

so each ED and SCBA array integrates to one over `x in [-18,18]`.  The figure
shows the honest restricted window `x in [-1.25,1.55]`; normalization is never
redone on that display window.  Exact curves are black solid and SCBA curves
are red dashed according to `Figures/common/si_plot_style.py`.
