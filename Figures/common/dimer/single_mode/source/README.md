# Two-site benchmark with independent undamped local modes

> **Historical production archive.**  The complete calculation documented
> below uses `eta/omega0=0.01` and is preserved unchanged for provenance.  It
> is no longer the active input to final Figs. S6--S7.  The current figures use the
> freshly recomputed, scale-matched `eta/nu0=0.005` profile generated by
> `../generate_eta0p005.py` and routed through `../data/source_manifest.json`.
> That active profile uses the preserved publication grid `Delta x=0.002`;
> its targeted half-step validation uses `Delta x=0.001`.

This directory compares exact diagonalization (ED), the one-shot Born
approximation (BA), and the self-consistent Born approximation (SCBA/NCA) for
a Frenkel dimer coupled to two independent, identical, undamped oscillators.
The current primary study is an intrinsic single-mode calculation at
\(T=0\): it holds the Huang--Rhys factor and \(\eta/\omega_0\) fixed while
scanning \(r=2|J|/\omega_0\).  It does not reuse continuum-bath parameters.

The result has two physically distinct parts.  First, site-local SCBA/NCA
already has a smooth Franck--Condon error at \(J=0\), because its continued
fraction does not recover the exact bosonic factors \(1,2,3,\ldots\).
Second, finite \(J\) adds commensurability structure when
\(2|J|/\omega_0\) approaches an integer.  Odd positive-\(J\) guides are
symmetry-allowed bright--dark conversion channels, whereas even integers are
parity-forbidden controls and are not exact avoided crossings.  The practical
benchmark conclusion is that the site-local closure is controlled most
reliably at weak or moderate \(S\) away from vibronic commensurabilities.
Positive-\(J\) resonance can amplify its error well above the irreducible
bare-molecule/local background, while increasing \(S\) raises that background
and widens the visibly inaccurate region.  The main quantitative measure is
the dimensionless first Wasserstein distance

\[
w=\frac{W_1(A_{\rm ED},A_{\rm SCBA})}{\omega_0},\qquad
W_1=\int dE\,|F_{\rm ED}(E)-F_{\rm SCBA}(E)|,
\]

evaluated after independently normalizing the ED and SCBA spectra over the
declared analysis window.  It measures global spectral transport; it does
not by itself identify peak topology, a particular pole error, or a unique
omitted diagram.

All code energies are in eV and \(\hbar=1\).  Stored spectra use

\[
A(\omega)=-\operatorname{Im}G^R(\omega)
\]

without \(1/\pi\), so a normalized projected Green function has full-line
area \(\pi\).  Shape metrics are computed only after the raw areas pass their
checks.

## Model and optical convention

In the one-excitation site basis,

\[
H=\epsilon_v\sum_{i=1}^2|i\rangle\langle i|
+J(|1\rangle\langle2|+|2\rangle\langle1|)
+\omega_0\sum_i b_i^\dagger b_i
+g\sum_i|i\rangle\langle i|(b_i+b_i^\dagger),
\]

with

\[
\lambda=g^2/\omega_0=S\omega_0,
\qquad g=\omega_0\sqrt S,
\qquad \epsilon_v=E_{00}+\lambda,
\qquad E_{00}=2.0\ \mathrm{eV}.
\]

Equal parallel normalized dipoles create

\[
|B\rangle=(|1\rangle+|2\rangle)/\sqrt2,
\qquad |D\rangle=(|1\rangle-|2\rangle)/\sqrt2,
\]

with bare energies \(E_B=\epsilon_v+J\) and \(E_D=\epsilon_v-J\).
Only \(-\operatorname{Im}G_B\) is called optical absorption.  Dark and local
projected spectra are stored under explicit names.

## Exact common/difference reduction and parity

With \(b_\pm=(b_1\pm b_2)/\sqrt2\),

\[
H_1=H_++H_-,
\]

\[
H_+=\omega_0b_+^\dagger b_+
+\frac{g}{\sqrt2}(b_++b_+^\dagger),
\]

\[
H_-=\epsilon_v I+J\tau_z+\omega_0b_-^\dagger b_-
+\frac{g}{\sqrt2}\tau_x(b_-+b_-^\dagger).
\]

The common mode is displaced analytically and has Huang--Rhys factor \(S/2\).
The difference mode also has effective factor \(S/2\) and converts bright and
dark excitons.  Its exact Rabi parity is

\[
\Pi=\tau_z(-1)^{N_-},\qquad [H_-,\Pi]=0.
\]

The parity assignments are
\(\Pi|B,n\rangle=(-1)^n|B,n\rangle\) and
\(\Pi|D,n\rangle=(-1)^{n+1}|D,n\rangle\).  Therefore \(|B,0\rangle\)
hybridizes with \(|D,n\rangle\) only for odd \(n\).  For \(J>0\), the ratios
\(r=1,3,5,\ldots\) are bare weak-coupling guides for avoided crossings
reached from \(|B,0\rangle\) by phonon emission.  For \(J<0\), the
symmetry-related degeneracy involves \(|B,n\rangle\) and \(|D,0\rangle\) and
is not reached from \(|B,0\rangle\) by zero-temperature emission.  Absolute
ratios 2 and 4 are even-parity controls, not exact two- or four-phonon
bright--dark avoided crossings.  Dressed centers shift with \(S\), and at
strong coupling a selected branch pair may not cross in the sampled interval.

At the isolated one-phonon crossing the direct matrix element is
\(g/\sqrt2\), while the weak two-level full gap is \(\sqrt2g\).  A necessary
off-resonance condition is

\[
|2J-\omega_0|\gg g/\sqrt2.
\]

Dipole asymmetry alone does not break this Hamiltonian parity; it changes the
optically prepared sector.  Site-energy, mode, or coupling asymmetry can
break it and open a previously forbidden gap.

## ED and SCBA implementations

The production ED factorizes the problem.  It treats the displaced common
mode analytically, diagonalizes only the \(2N_-\)-dimensional difference-mode
Hamiltonian, convolves the two pole measures, and applies the Lorentzian
width afterward.  The difference cutoff is adapted using pole-weight,
first-moment, level, and broadened-spectrum convergence.  A small
rotation-invariant total-phonon-shell calculation checks the equivalence to
the two-local-mode representation.

The site-local Fan closure is

\[
\mathbf G^R=[(\omega+i\eta)I-H_{\rm ex}
-\operatorname{diag}(\Sigma_1,\Sigma_2)]^{-1},
\]

\[
\Sigma_i=g^2\{(n_0+1)G_{ii}(\omega-\omega_0)
+n_0G_{ii}(\omega+\omega_0)\}.
\]

For the symmetric dimer,

\[
G_{B,D}=[\omega-\epsilon_v\mp J-\Sigma+i\eta]^{-1},
\qquad G_{\rm loc}=(G_B+G_D)/2,
\]

and the skeleton uses \(G_{\rm loc}\), not the optically bright propagator
alone.  BA applies the map once to the bare Green function.  SCBA iterates it
with adaptive damping.  At \(T=0\) an independent ascending-frequency sweep
solves the causal one-way recursion.  A full asymmetric two-site solve checks
the exchange-symmetric reduction.

The retarded \(\eta\) is a spectral-resolution and Green-function
regularization width, including on SCBA internal lines.  It is not a phonon
decay rate and does not shorten the oscillator memory.

## Why local NCA fails

At \(J=T=0\), SCBA produces a continued fraction with the same numerator
\(g^2\) at every rung.  The exact independent-boson fraction contains
\(g^2,2g^2,3g^2,\ldots\).  NCA is correct at the first \(O(g^2)\) rung but
misses bosonic multiphonon combinatorics beginning at \(O(g^4)\).  This is a
local vertex error present before the sites are coupled.

The exact weight beyond one phonon is
\(1-e^{-S}(1+S)\), equal to 0.0902, 0.2642, and 0.5940 for
\(S=0.5,1,2\).  Large \(|J|\) suppresses difference-mode conversion but
leaves the common-mode problem with \(S_{\rm eff}=S/2\).

## Fixed-\(S\) frequency scan

The primary scan holds

- \(|J|=25\) meV and both signs of \(J\);
- \(T=0\), \(E_{00}=2.0\) eV, and \(\eta/\omega_0=0.01\);
- \(S=(0.02,0.05,0.10,0.25,0.50,1,2)\); and
- \(r=2|J|/\omega_0\) from 0.25 to 5.5.

Varying \(r\) at fixed \(|J|\) varies \(\omega_0=50/r\) meV.  Because the
scan holds \(S\), not \(g\) or \(\lambda\), fixed, both
\(g=\omega_0\sqrt S\) and \(\lambda=S\omega_0\) change along every curve.
This is a dimensionless commensurability scan, not a fixed-\(g\) or
fixed-\(\lambda\) scan.

The command-15 mesh is the union of the complete command-14 mesh and new
targeted refinement.  It contains 369 measured \(r\) values for every
\(S\)/sign group: the original 0.1 grid, the original 0.01 odd-guide
shoulders, and 61 points with \(\Delta r=0.005\) on
\([n-0.15,n+0.15]\) for every integer \(n=1,\ldots,5\).  No interpolation
or smoothing is used to create the plotted cusp shapes.  The common spectral
grid is \(x=(E-E_B)/\omega_0\in[-18,18]\), with internal range
\([-32,24]\) and \(\Delta x=0.002\).

The secondary scan fixes \(\lambda=30\) meV and \(|J|=25\) meV.  Since
\(S=\lambda/\omega_0=0.6r\) varies in that scan, it mixes the local
Franck--Condon background with finite-\(J\) commensurability and is not used
to define the central result.

Python 3.8 or newer with NumPy, SciPy, and Matplotlib is required.  From this
directory, a full deterministic rebuild is:

```bash
python3 exact_level_map.py --overwrite
python3 run_frequency_scan.py --stage production --checkpoint 100
python3 plot_frequency_scan.py
python3 audit_frequency_scan.py --check-only
python3 audit_frequency_scan.py
cd ../../notes
latexmk.exe -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error -file-line-error single_mode_dimer_scba_theory.tex
```

The command above uses the MiKTeX executable available in this WSL workspace;
on a native TeX installation, use `latexmk` in place of `latexmk.exe`.

`run_frequency_scan.py` writes checkpoint tables atomically.  Without
`--overwrite`, it skips completed cases with matching definition keys and
computes only missing points or archives.  Do not run two scan processes
against the same manifest.  To rerun one selected archive safely:

```bash
python3 run_frequency_scan.py --stage primary \
  --case-id primary_S0p1_Jp_r0p8 --checkpoint 1
```

```bash
python3 calculate_single_mode_ed.py --validate-only
```

## Exact level-map results

The level generator uses \(J=+25\) meV and diagonalizes the two parity blocks separately.  The
stored commutator and forbidden off-block coupling are zero; the largest
eigenstate parity leakage is \(2.78\times10^{-15}\).

For \(S=0.10\), the first/third/fifth odd avoided-crossing gaps and fitted
centers are

- 0.440982 at \(r=0.94594\);
- 0.0139596 at \(r=2.84824\);
- 0.00019235 at \(r=4.87459\),

all in units of \(\omega_0\).  The one-phonon gap is 0.986 times the weak
two-level full gap \(2g/\sqrt2\).  The corresponding \(S=0.50\) centers shift
to 0.61599, 2.22988, and 4.36288.  At \(S=1\), branch reorganization makes
the first tracked minimum ambiguous, so the bare integer labels are only
guides.

Targeted opposite-parity controls cross with no avoided gap: at \(S=0.10\),
the tracked B0/D2 and B0/D4 pairs cross near 1.788854 and 3.865866 with gaps
below \(5.6\times10^{-16}\omega_0\).  At \(S=0.50\), the tracked D4 pair
crosses near 3.305783, while the D2 target pair has no root in the scanned
range.  Symmetry forbids level repulsion but does not force a chosen dressed
pair to cross.

## Interpretation and limits

The Hilbert-space/pseudoparticle representation is not the approximation
being tested.  The approximation is the site-local two-vertex Fan/NCA
closure.  An undamped oscillator has infinite memory and resolved
Franck--Condon structure; it is not a generally favorable slow- or fast-bath
limit.  SCBA is most accurate when the smooth local Franck--Condon background
is perturbatively small and the optically prepared exciton is away from
symmetry-allowed odd conversion channels.  This is a crossover statement,
not a universal threshold in \(S\).

Large splitting leaves the common-mode \(S/2\) problem.  Increasing \(\eta\)
may define a useful coarse-grained observable, but it does not restore omitted
diagrams.  ED measures the total NCA error; it cannot uniquely assign that
error to one crossing diagram.  A one-crossing/OCA or vertex-corrected
calculation is needed for that decomposition.
